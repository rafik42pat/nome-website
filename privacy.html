Privacy policy

This policy explains how NOMĒ ("we") collects and uses personal data when you visit nomeparfum.com or contact us. We handle personal data in line with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).

What we collect

  • Personalisation preview: names and messages you type into the band preview stay in your browser. They are not sent to us unless you choose to send them to us on WhatsApp.
  • Enquiries: details you send through our corporate or partner forms, by WhatsApp or by email, such as your name, company, email, phone number and any logo you share.
  • Priority List: your email address and country, and a record that you agreed to receive email updates.
  • Analytics: if you accept cookies, we use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics and Meta Pixel) to understand how visitors use the site. These are not loaded if you decline.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide quotes.
  • To produce personalised bands for gifts that have been ordered.
  • If you join the Priority List, to email you about upcoming releases, collection availability and launches. We only send these emails with your consent.
  • To improve our website and products.

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business (for example, our printing partner for personalised bands, our website, form and email providers), and with our gifting partner where needed to fulfil an order.

Unsubscribing

Every Priority List email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to remove you at any time by contacting . We remove your email address from our list when you unsubscribe.

How long we keep it

We keep Priority List details until you unsubscribe. We keep enquiry and order details only as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal and accounting requirements.
