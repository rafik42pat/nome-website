FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is in each set?

Two 100ml Eau de Parfum fragrances, one for the day and one for the night, in our premium NOMĒ presentation box.

Which set should I choose?

For Him pairs No.1 Black Birch and No.2 Amber Leaf. For Her pairs No.3 Rose Petal and No.4 Saffron Amber. Night for Two pairs No.2 Amber Leaf with No.4 Saffron Amber, for him and for her.

Is personalisation included?

Yes. The recipient's name, an occasion band and your message card are included in the AED 399 price. You can also choose our standard presentation with the NOMĒ Signature band.

Can I write the name and message in Arabic?

Yes. Names, band messages and message cards can be written in English or Arabic.

What does Limited Monthly Release mean?

We release no more than 2,500 Day & Night Collection sets each month. When a month's release has been allocated, you can join the Priority List for the next one. The collection itself returns every month.

Where can I buy NOMĒ?

NOMĒ launches in the UAE through our exclusive gifting partner, with wider GCC availability to follow. Join the Priority List to hear when the next release opens.

Does joining the Priority List reserve a set?

No. The Priority List lets you know first when a release opens. It does not reserve or guarantee a set.

Can I return a personalised gift?

Personalised sets are made to order, so they can only be returned if they arrive damaged or incorrect. See our returns policy.

Do you offer corporate gifting?

Yes. Visit Corporate Gifting to tell us about your quantities, dates and branding.
