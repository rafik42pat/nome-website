Our Story

Thoughtfully Gifted. Beautifully Remembered.

Two distinctive fragrances. One beautifully presented gift.

NOMĒ began with a simple belief: the most memorable gifts are the ones that feel personal. A fragrance becomes part of how someone is remembered, and the way it is given matters just as much.

The Day & Night Collection pairs two complementary 100ml Eau de Parfum fragrances: one for the energy of the day, another for the character of the evening. Each pairing is presented in our signature gift box and can be made personal with the recipient's name, an occasion band and your own words.

The NOMĒ Philosophy

Distinctive by nature. Considered by design.

NOMĒ brings together two expressions of fragrance in one carefully curated collection.

Created for the moments that define your day and the occasions that make your evenings memorable.

Each collection contains two 100ml Eau de Parfum fragrances, presented in NOMĒ's signature packaging, with complimentary personalisation available for gifting.

A UAE brand, made for gifting.

NOMĒ is a UAE brand, launching first in the UAE. Every detail, from the black-and-silver box to the message card inside, is there to make giving feel effortless and receiving feel special.
