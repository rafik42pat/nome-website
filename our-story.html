Our Story

A fragrance for every side of you.

At NOMĒ, we believe fragrance is more than something you wear. It becomes part of how you are remembered.

The Day & Night Collection was created around a simple idea: different moments deserve different expressions. One fragrance for the energy and confidence of the day. Another for the character and atmosphere of the evening.

Each collection brings together two distinctive 100ml Eau de Parfum fragrances, presented with care and designed to be enjoyed personally or given as a meaningful gift.

Considered in every detail.

From the distinctive fragrance compositions to the presentation of each collection, NOMĒ is guided by an appreciation for simplicity, elegance and detail. Crafted in the UAE, NOMĒ reflects a contemporary approach to fragrance, where individuality and presentation are equally important.

Exclusivity, by design.

NOMĒ does not pursue unlimited availability. Our Day & Night Collection is limited to a maximum of 2,500 sets per month. Not as a passing promotion, but as a deliberate approach to how the collection is offered.
