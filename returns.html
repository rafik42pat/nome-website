Help

Returns & refunds

NOMĒ gift boxes are currently sold through our exclusive gifting partner. Returns and refunds for those orders are handled under the partner's returns policy, so please contact them first.

Personalised and opened items

Because each band is printed with a name and message, and perfume is a personal-care product, personalised or opened items can usually only be returned if they arrive damaged, faulty or incorrect.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your gift box arrives damaged, or the personalisation is not what was ordered, please take photos and contact the partner within 48 hours of delivery. You can also contact us on WhatsApp or at and we will help.

Nothing in this policy affects your rights under UAE consumer protection law.
